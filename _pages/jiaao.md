Jiaao Yu is a PhD candidate in RCSL, TCD, working on the [DISCLOSE](/projects/disclose/) project (Distributed Sensing and Collaborative Optimisation for Smart Energy-efficient Buildings). The research explores how everyday household devices can collaboratively monitor and optimise their electricity usage without relying on a centralised controller or sharing personal data with external parties. The key questions are the design of lightweight distributed consensus mechanisms that let connected devices securely aggregate consumption data and agree on optimal control schedules in real time, while adapting to human-driven interactions and overrides, using only the existing compute and wireless capabilities of modern smart-home devices.
